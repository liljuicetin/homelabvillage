WHY WE EXIST

We are always told the best way to get hands-on cybersecurity experience is to build a home lab. That advice is solid, but the first step can feel like staring over the edge of a very deep rabbit hole.

Home Lab Village exists to make that step easier. It is a place to share projects, compare notes, collaborate, and learn in public without pretending you already know everything.

Whether you run an advanced Proxmox cluster, are setting up your first Raspberry Pi, or have never touched a terminal, you are welcome here.
