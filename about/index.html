ABOUT HOME LAB VILLAGE

About

A place to share projects, collaborate, and learn together.

We're always told that the best way to get hands on cybersecurity experience is with our own home lab, but the home lab rabbit hole can be scary for those unfamiliar with it. That is why I started this community, to give people a place to share any projects they are working on, collaborate, and learn together.

In this community, it doesn't matter whether you are an advanced Proxmox user, just starting in homelabbing on a Raspberry Pi, or have never touched a terminal, all are welcome here.

The Home Lab Village is a volunteer-run community, and we're always happy to have like-minded individuals join our team to help build this community. If you're interested in volunteering, contributing to a project, helping at an event, or getting more involved, feel free to reach out at
